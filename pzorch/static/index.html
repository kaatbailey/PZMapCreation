New project

Becomes the folder under ~/Zomboid/mods and the name you pick in PZ. Letters, digits and underscore only.

Pipeline —

0
Compile
javac -encoding UTF-8 -d out over every .java. Only after you change the generator.
1
Area
Paste the GeoJSON boundary.
2
Fetch GIS
pzgis pulls buildings + roads into the project. Minutes for a large area.
3
Generate (provisional)
Builds the mod at origin 200,200 so you have something to place. maxTiles=0 always.
4
Render tiles
main.py render base_top. Seconds. Never touches the base layer.
5
Place on the world
Drag the overlay, hit Apply, then Send to Orchestrator — the origin lands back here.
6
Build at the real origin
Same generator call with the placed origin, then re-render. Spawn points are re-applied afterwards.
7
Spawn points
Pin them on the mod map. Saved to the project and re-applied after every build.
